LAUNDRY APPLIANCE SERVICE

Common problems we service

Laundry equipment combines water, electricity, heat and moving components. A careful diagnosis helps identify the actual source of the problem before parts are replaced.

  • Washer will not drain or spin
  • Leaks, vibration or excessive movement
  • Machine will not start or complete a cycle
  • Dryer runs but does not heat
  • Clothes take too long to dry
  • Unusual noise or control-panel errors
